Research

I study linguistic meaning using formal, experimental, and diachronic methods. My empirical focus is on Mandarin and Classical Chinese, alongside cross-linguistic work.

Much of my recent work concerns negation: expletive negation in Mandarin (e.g., negation under yiqian 'before' interacting with Aktionsart and grammatical aspect); the processing of meta-linguistic negation drawing on experimental evidence from negated tautologies.

A second strand concerns diachronic formal semantics. With Paloma Jeretič, I have been investigating the light verb modal dé (meaning 'to get; to obtain' when used as a lexical verb) in Chinese, and we have been building structured historical corpora that track the development of dé across historical stages of Chinese.

I also work on definiteness from a cross-linguistic perspective: two types of definites in Kono, based on my own fieldwork, and the definite system of Mandarin.
